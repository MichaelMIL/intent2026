# INTENT 2026 badge: schematic/BOM review vs RP2040 reference design (2026-10-17)

Reference: Raspberry Pi *Hardware design with RP2040* (RP-008279-DS), chapter 2 "Minimal design example".
Tooling: kicad-happy @ 6a91aaa (analyze_schematic, analyze_pcb --full, cross_analysis, analyze_emc,
analyze_thermal, analyze_gerbers) run on the working-tree schematic (includes the C10/C18/C26 edit, see §0)
and the committed rev 0.5 PCB / gerbers.

## Verdict

Not ready to order. The RP2040 core matches the reference except for the VREG decoupling (now fixed in the
schematic, not yet on the PCB) and the crystal choice. The real design risks are on the power side: the
WS2812 LEDs on VSYS (supply range and data level) and the 3V3 regulator current budget. Two new items from
kicad-happy are worth acting on: no capacitor on VBUS, and USBLC6 VBUS pin undecoupled.

## 0. State of the files (updated after the user's decisions)

All items below except TP1–TP3 (kept) were applied to `design.py` / `gen_sch.py` and the schematic was
regenerated: `check_sch.py --erc` 317 pins, 79 nets, 0 problems, ERC 0 errors / 0 warnings. BOM changes are in
`00-bom/bom-deltas.md` (rev 0.6) and the Hebrew mirror. RGB went with the buffer (SN74LV1T34, C100024), because no
LCSC/JLC addressable LED is rated for a 3.0–3.3 V supply. The PCB is not re-laid out yet (BUILD-NOTES item 14).

## 2. Your list, item by item

- **USB-C → HRO TYPE-C-31-M-12, LCSC C165948**: agree. 16P USB 2.0, $0.10 @1k, 348k stock. Needs a footprint
  in `gen_fp.py` from the EasyEDA data, J1 re-placement and local re-route. The current J1 courtyard overhangs
  the edge by 0.54 mm (PM-002); recheck after the swap. CC 5.1 k ×2, 27 Ω series, USBLC6 all match the guide §2.4.1.
- **U3 → AP2112K-3.3**: agree, see item 4.
- **Expansion ports → SAO**: SAO = 2×3 2.54 mm, keyed female socket on the badge, shrouded male header on the
  add-on. Pins 1 3V3, 2 GND, 3 SDA, 4 SCL, 5 GPIO1, 6 GPIO2. Implications: shared I²C bus + pull-ups (e.g. 4.7 k)
  on the badge, GPIO1/GPIO2 per port (IR_OUT / AUDIO_PWM / SPK_PWM become GPIO1), 2×3 headers on the 13 × 22 mm
  cans (fits), re-route of the module area. Free I²C-capable pins: GPIO0/1 (I2C0) if TP1–3 go, or GPIO12/13,
  GPIO20/21. Spec also suggests an ID EEPROM at 0x50 on add-ons (optional for ours).
- **RGB at 3.0 V**: see item 3.
- **TP1–TP3**: not needed for users. stdio goes over USB CDC; UART0 only matters for low-level debug, and J2
  already gives SWD. They cost nothing in BOM (THT pads, not populated) but use board area. Recommend removing
  them and using GPIO0/1 as the SAO I²C bus.
- **C10 → 100 nF**: agree, done (§0).
- **VREG_VIN / VREG_VOUT decoupling**: agree, done (§0).
- **Crystal → ABM8-272-T3**: yes, switch (item 2). Drop-in footprint and it makes the existing load caps correct.

## 3. Other checks vs the reference (match unless noted)

- IOVDD 100 nF ×6, USB_VDD 100 nF, ADC_AVDD 100 nF, DVDD 100 nF ×2: match (guide even shares one cap on 48/49).
- TESTEN (pin 19) → GND; QSPI_SS 10 k pull-up + 1 k to BOOTSEL: match (guide fits the 10 k as DNF).
- RUN: 10 k pull-up, **no reset button**. On battery, entering BOOTSEL requires removing USB and batteries.
  Suggest a second TS-1187A (C318884) from RUN to GND.
- Flash W25Q16JV (2 MB) on QSPI: fine.
- USB D+/D− skew 7.5 mm and layer changes (DP-001/003): the 25 ps limit is for High Speed; irrelevant at
  12 Mbit/s full speed. False positive.
